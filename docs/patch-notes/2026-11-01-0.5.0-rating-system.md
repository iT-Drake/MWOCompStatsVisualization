# 0.5.0 - Rating system introduction

After a year of work, a rating system for competitive play was finished.

Please welcome:
### **S.O.P.A. Score** = **S**urvival **O**ptimized **P**ilot **A**ssessment*
- **S** = **Survival Factor:** A score modifier rewarding battlefield longevity and penalizing early ejections.
- **O** = **Optimized Match Score:** Multi-tiered percentile ranking scaled against division-wide Mech performance benchmarks.
- **P** = **Precision Impact:** High-value combat actions, prioritizing "Kills Most Damage Dealt" (KMDD) and Components Destroyed over raw damage.
- **P** = **Precision Impact:** A dynamic rating scaler based on the competitive strength of your opponents.

*unofficially known as "Sense of Pride and Accomplishment"
